# Toy Elementary — synthetic MSBA-inspired fixture

~23 spaces, 15 programs, Schemes A (good) and B (planted problems).

| Scheme | Intent |
|--------|--------|
| A | Grade neighborhoods, commons by Grade 4, art by Grade 5 |
| B | Undersized far art, fringe commons (fails Grade 4 hard nearby), longer travel |

Used by CLI: `spi evaluate --fixture toy_elementary --scheme A`
